# Sales Lead Analysis

## Project Summary

This analysis reviews a sample sales lead dataset to demonstrate how sales information can be organized and used to identify opportunities for follow-up and conversion.

## Key Observations

- The dataset contains 8 sample leads.
- Leads come from Website, Referral, and Social Media channels.
- Leads are tracked across different stages including New, Qualified, Follow-Up, Converted, and Lost.
- Follow-up dates are recorded to help ensure leads are contacted on time.
- Estimated values are included to support sales pipeline planning.

## Sales Process

The lead tracking process follows these stages:

1. New Lead
2. Qualification
3. Follow-Up
4. Conversion
5. Closed/Lost

## Business Recommendations

- Prioritize qualified leads for immediate follow-up.
- Monitor leads approaching their follow-up dates.
- Compare lead sources to identify which channels generate the strongest opportunities.
- Keep CRM information accurate and up to date.
- Review lost leads to identify possible areas for improvement.
